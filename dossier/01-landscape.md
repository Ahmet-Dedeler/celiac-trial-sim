# The 2026 Landscape

Status as of **August 2026**. No FDA-approved drug exists for celiac disease. The gluten-free
diet remains the sole standard of care. ~25–30 companies have programs; roughly 30 candidates
are in the pipeline.

---

## Arm A — Tolerance induction (the only "cure-shaped" approach)

Goal: re-educate or delete the gluten-specific CD4+ T cells so gluten stops being seen as a
threat. **Track record: unbroken failure at the level that matters.**

| Candidate | Sponsor | Mechanism | Stage | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Nexvax2** | ImmusanT | 3-peptide therapeutic vaccine | Ph2 | **Halted 2019.** Missed primary endpoint; arguably made symptoms worse |
| **TAK-101** | Takeda (ex-COUR) | Gliadin in tolerogenic PLGA nanoparticle | Ph2 (n=102) | **Discontinued Jan 2026.** Ph2a had been spectacular on biomarkers — 88% cut in gliadin-specific IFN-γ⁺ cells — and it still didn't translate |
| **KAN-101 / Kanyos** | Anokion (→ Kanyos Bio) | Liver-targeted glycosylated gluten peptide | Ph2 ACeD-it + Ph2a SynCeD | **Shelved.** Positive symptom data, "discontinued for economic reasons"; Anokion reportedly wound down. Partnered with Pfizer Ignite |
| **TPM502** | Topas Therapeutics | Iron-oxide nanoparticle → liver sinusoidal endothelium | Ph2a done (DDW 2025) | Dose-dependent drop in gluten-specific IL-2/IFN-γ, ↑ gluten-specific Tregs, symptom trend. **Biomarker win, no histology win** |
| **VTP-1000** | Barinthus Bio | SNAP-TI nanoparticle: gluten peptides + rapamycin | Ph1 AVALON | SAD safe, dose-dependent IL-2 response. MAD + gluten challenge data **H2 2026** |
| **eTregs (TCR-edited)** | Porret et al., academic | Orthotopic TCR replacement in Tregs, DQ2.5-glia-α1a/α2 specific | Mouse | Works in vivo, bystander suppression. **No sponsor, no clinic** |

**Pattern to notice:** every one of these moves the blood biomarkers (IL-2, IFN-γ ELISpot) and
none of them protects the intestine. That is not a coincidence — see
[02-why-no-cure.md](02-why-no-cure.md).

## Arm B — Effector blockade (chronic drug, not a cure)

Goal: leave the T cells alone; block the machinery that actually destroys the villi.
**Track record: this is where the wins are.**

| Candidate | Sponsor | Mechanism | Stage | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **TAK-227 / ZED1227** | Takeda + Zedira + Dr. Falk | Oral covalent TG2 inhibitor — blocks deamidation | Ph2b, Part 2 **re-enrolling 2026** | NEJM 2021: attenuated gluten-induced mucosal injury at all doses. Ph2b Part 1: **histology improved at 50mg QD, symptoms did not.** Takeda's last remaining celiac asset |
| **FB102** | Forte Bio → **argenx (acquired Jul 2026)** | Anti-CD122 (IL-2Rβ/IL-15Rβ) — blocks IL-2 *and* IL-15 signaling | Ph1b done, Ph2 recruiting | **Best histology data in the field.** VCIEL change −1.849 placebo vs +0.079 drug (p=0.0099); IEL CD3 density +13.3 vs −1.5 (p=0.0035); 42% symptom benefit. FDA Fast Track May 2026 |
| **TEV-53408** | Teva | Anti-IL-15 mAb, quarterly dosing | Ph2a (NCT06806... TEV-CeD2), n≈48 | Fast Track May 2025. Ph1 cut free IL-15 + NK counts. **Topline H2 2026** |
| **PRV-015 / AMG 714 (ordesekimab)** | Provention → Sanofi | Anti-IL-15 mAb | Ph2b done | Reduced intestinal inflammation, **missed primary endpoints** |
| **Amlitelimab** | Sanofi | Anti-OX40L | Ph2 recruiting | No celiac data yet; strong in atopic dermatitis |
| **Ritlecitinib** | Pfizer | JAK3/TEC inhibitor | Ph2 recruiting | Post-hoc: dropped celiac antibody titres in vitiligo/alopecia patients |
| **IMU-856** | Immunic | SIRT6 agonist — restores epithelial regeneration | Ph1b done | Safe, modest architecture effect |

## Arm C — Luminal / keep gluten from ever being seen

| Candidate | Sponsor | Mechanism | Stage | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Larazotide acetate** | 9 Meters | Tight-junction modulator | **Ph3 failed** | No benefit vs placebo. Program dead |
| **TAK-062 (zamaglutenase)** | Takeda (ex-PvP Bio, $330M) | Engineered gluten endopeptidase | **Ph2 discontinued** | Failed symptoms *and* villous height:crypt depth **got worse** than placebo |
| **Latiglutenase (IMGX-003)** | ImmunogenX | Enzyme cocktail (EP-B2 + SC PEP) | Ph2 | No mucosal healing or symptom relief in non-responders |
| **UB-Barcelona glutenase** | academic (2026) | Designed molecule active at **pH 2**, low concentration | Preclinical | First to work in real stomach acid — previously the blocker |
| **CRISPR low-gliadin wheat** | Multiple (CSIC Córdoba, others) | Multiplex KO of α/γ/ω-gliadin gene families | Field trials | Up to **97.7% gluten reduction**; Δgli-D2 lines even bake *better*. **Not celiac-safe yet** — A and B genome epitopes remain |

## What changed in the last 12 months

1. **Takeda retreated to a single asset.** TAK-062 dropped, TAK-101 dropped (Jan 2026). Only
   TAK-227 survives. Big pharma's read on tolerance induction is now visibly negative.
2. **argenx bought into celiac** (Forte/FB102, July 2026). A serious antibody company entering
   with the best histology package is the most bullish signal in the field.
3. **The IL-2 release assay matured.** Whole-blood gluten-stimulated IL-2 now diagnoses celiac
   *without* a gluten challenge (Gastroenterology 2025), and plasma IL-2 rises within ~4h of
   gluten ingestion — the earliest, most sensitive marker known. This is the field's most
   underexploited tool.
4. **First engineered-cell therapy concept published** (Sci Transl Med, Mar 2025): TCR-edited
   gluten-specific Tregs suppress effector T cells in vivo, including bystander epitopes.
5. **Celiac Disease Foundation launched a $15M venture fund** (Impact Fund, Mar 2026, with
   Triple G Ventures) — the first dedicated capital pool in this disease.
